Morning 10

Seven 10-minute routines for before a physical day. Kit: TRX, mat, small kettlebell. Rate each strength move after a session and the plan adjusts it next time: harder, easier or longer.

Looking after the shoulders
  • Pain is the guide, not the crunch. Clicking without pain is usually harmless. Mild discomfort (2–3 out of 10) that settles straight away is fine; sharp or lingering pain means rate it too hard and it'll step down.
  • Mon · Wed · Fri strengthen the muscles that steady the shoulder blade. Tue · Thu load the legs and core and give the shoulders a rest.
  • Nothing is loaded overhead. Side-lying rotations want a very light weight (a water bottle, or anything under ~4 kg).
  • See a physio if pain lasts beyond a few weeks, wakes you at night, or you notice weakness.
How it gets harder
  • Each strength move has a ladder of 3–4 versions. You can see where you are on its dots.
  • Too easy → next version up. At the top version, work time goes up instead (45 → 50 → 55 s).
  • Too hard → less work time first, then down a version.
  • The circuit always runs in 1-minute blocks, so more work time means less rest and the session stays 10 minutes.
  • Rate during the timer, on the summary at the end, or with the ‹ easier / harder › buttons on any card.
Making flexibility actually improve
  • Little and often wins. Give it 6–8 weeks.
  • Contract-relax: in a stretch, push gently against it for 5 s, relax, ease a little deeper. Repeat 2–3 times.
  • Saturday is your full flexibility session. Keep doing it even when progress feels slow.
